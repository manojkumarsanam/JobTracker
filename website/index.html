Everything a serious job search needs

Built for people sending hundreds of tailored applications who never want to lose track of what they told which company.

⌨️

One-hotkey logging

Press ⌥⇧J anywhere — even with the app closed to the tray — and a popup captures company, role, salary, portal, and your documents in seconds.

📄

LaTeX-smart document storage

Paste your .tex source: kilobytes per entry instead of megabytes, compiled to PDF only when you view it. Prefer PDFs? Attach those instead — your choice, per document.

📊

A dashboard you design

GitHub-style activity calendar, momentum, forecasts, funnel, portal effectiveness, a world map — hide what you don't need, drag to rearrange, and build your own charts: bar, pie, histogram, box plot, KDE.

🎯

Goals & forecasts

Set a target — with or without a deadline — and see your projected finish date, the pace you need, and whether you're on track.

🔁

Status funnel

Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer. Colored badges, hover to update, every transition timestamped for honest response-rate analytics.

🤖

Local AI assistant

Connect your own Ollama and ask "which portal responds most?" in plain language. Locked to your data — it refuses everything else — and nothing leaves your machine.

🧩

Your fields, your form

Add, rename, reorder, hide, or require any field. Removing one never deletes the data you already saved.

📤

One-click export

CSV or Excel with every field you ever used. Your data is never locked in.

🔍

Anomaly detection

Unusual spikes or drops in your activity get flagged — add your own "why" and it's saved with the data.

How it works

From "I just applied" to "fully recorded" in under ten seconds.

Apply anywhere

Finish submitting an application on any site, in any browser.

Press the hotkey

The popup appears over whatever you're doing. Fill in the details, paste your .tex or attach the PDF.

Hit save

Entry stored, documents filed, dashboard updated. Back to the search.

Watch the insights

Streaks, forecasts, portal win-rates, and more accumulate automatically as you go.

🔒 Private by design

No account. No cloud. No telemetry. Everything lives in one SQLite database inside a folder you choose — move the folder and your data moves with it. The optional AI assistant talks only to your own Ollama, normally on localhost. The code is open source, so you don't have to take our word for it.

Install in a minute

Grab the latest installer from GitHub Releases. The app is unsigned (no $99/yr certificate) — your OS will ask once; here's how to answer.

macOS

  1. Download the .dmg and drag Job Tracker into Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click the app → Open → Open. (Or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.) Needed once only.
  3. Optional, for .tex previews: brew install tectonic

🪟 Windows

  1. Download and run the .msi installer.
  2. If SmartScreen appears: More info → Run anyway. Needed once only.
  3. Optional, for .tex previews: winget install TectonicProject.Tectonic

Uninstalling (just as clean)

Job Tracker keeps everything in three places — remove them and it's gone without a trace:

  • The app — macOS: drag it from Applications to Trash · Windows: Settings → Apps → Uninstall.
  • Your data folder — the folder you picked at setup (your database and documents). Keep it if you might come back; export to CSV/Excel first if you want a copy.
  • App preferences — macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/JobTracker · Windows: %APPDATA%\JobTracker. A single small file remembering where your data folder is.
